    What is in it

    Original: beef, your choice of pork or lamb fat, salt, black pepper, cloves, nutmeg, roasted coriander, Worcestershire sauce and water, in a natural casing.

    Cheese wors: the same, plus diced cheese.

    Gluten free: the same, with the Worcestershire sauce replaced by vinegar.

    Fat content: 30%, from the pork or lamb fat you choose.

    Allergens: the Worcestershire sauce contains fish (anchovy) and gluten (barley malt vinegar). Cheese wors contains milk.

    Thin wors uses sheep casing and cooks fast. Thick uses hog casing and suits the braai.
